## 1. Ownership & Control — Both Sides, Fully Protected

**Side A — The Inventor (Personal):**
All intellectual property in the SQUIRL OS ecosystem — every patent application, every provisional, every method, every template, every recipe — is owned **solely and entirely by Leon Calvin Long II**, filed in his personal name. This is deliberate doctrine: patents are filed in the inventor's personal capacity, exactly as the United States Patent and Trademark Office records them. No filing, license, sale, or partnership transfers, dilutes, or encumbers that ownership.

**Side B — The Company (Commercial):**
SquirlOS Technologies LLC holds an **exclusive commercial license** to market, distribute, sublicense, and sell USE of the IP. The LLC may grant customers use rights at any tier. The LLC may never sell, assign, or transfer ownership of the underlying IP. This structure protects both sides permanently: the inventor cannot lose the IP through company debt, dissolution, or dispute; the company cannot be blocked from commercializing it.

**Both sides rest on one rule:**
> Ownership stays with the inventor. Use rights flow through the company. Neither side can be separated from its protection by the actions of the other.
