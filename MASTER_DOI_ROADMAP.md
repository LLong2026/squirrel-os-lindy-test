# MASTER DOI ROADMAP

**The single roadmap: every live DOI, what it is, and where it lives.** Current canonical record per work, plus every distribution surface (site + URL) and the local file location in this repo. Maintained append-only per the Lindy open-book doctrine; superseded versions stay accessible via Zenodo and are listed in the "Preserved versions" table. Companion to [RESEARCH_LINKS.md](RESEARCH_LINKS.md) (the full research hub with notes per record).
